My Story

A Voice Raised in Worship

Minister Princess Molwantwa is a gifted gospel artist whose ministry spans churches, weddings, funerals, concerts and community events across South Africa. Through my music, she carries a message of hope, faith and God's glory.

She is also the founder of Princess Music Studio, a creative home where artists record, grow and are launched into their calling.
